The axis scale completes a full turn every 180°, so an angle outside the interval shown below looks odd; that is why adding or subtracting 180 changes the name — never the axis.
=IF((C+K)>185,(C+K)−180, IF((C+K)<5,(C+K)+180,(C+K)))
The folded value is sent to the end system: that is where the official result resides and where the PASS/FAIL decision is made. Why [5, 185]? Every good lens lives there: targets 10 through 180, deviation ±5. It’s the only 180-wide window that cuts none of them — and the one that preserves the 181, 182… convention for the 180 SKU.
All four steps share a single building block: “fold this onto the short path.” In step 2 the vector sum does it; in step 3, MOD arithmetic; in step 4, the same fold but onto the reporting window.
